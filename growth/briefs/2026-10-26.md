## One experiment

**The "reply with your answer" test in WhatsApp groups.** For five days, post the daily WhatsApp question in two or three study groups you're already a member of, and ask people to reply with their answer before clicking. Post the answer the next morning. Count the replies by hand each day.

- **Why:** we can't measure clicks yet (no analytics), but replies are visible and are the earliest sign that someone would enter a contest.
- **Success:** 5 or more replies in a day by Friday. If that happens, the same groups are the first place to invite people to Alpha Hunt.
- **Cost:** about two minutes a day.
