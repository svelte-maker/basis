# LinkedIn kit

Two pieces: a **Company Page** (the brand, where you post) and a **Group** (where members talk). Create the Page first; the Group can be linked to it.

## 1. Company Page

### Create it

1. On LinkedIn, click **For Business** (top right, the grid icon) → **Create a Company Page** → **Company**.
2. Fill in the fields below, upload the logo, tick the verification box and click **Create page**.
3. On the new page, click **Edit page** and add the cover image, the About text and the website button ("Visit website", pointing to the site).
4. Add yourself as **Super admin** (you already are) and, if you like, a second admin you trust.
5. From your personal profile, add Basis as your current position ("Founder, Basis") so the page shows on your profile.

### About (overview)

> Basis is a free practice and rating platform for finance skills.
>
> Solve auto-graded problems across valuation, corporate finance, accounting, fixed income, derivatives, private equity, portfolio and risk, and banking and credit. Every member gets their own randomized numbers, so answers can't be copied.
>
> Compete in weekly timed contests and earn a rating from B to AAA, graded like a credit rating. Your profile has a public verification link you can put on your resume, so a recruiter can check your rating in one click.
>
> Practice with built-in tools: DCF, LBO, WACC and beta, trading comps, bond pricing, Black-Scholes and binomial options, NPV/IRR, VaR.
>
> Basis is new and free. Join a contest, tell us what's missing, and help shape it.
>
> Weekly contest: Sundays 17:00 IST (11:30 UTC).

## 2. LinkedIn Group

### Create it

1. On the left of your LinkedIn home feed, click **Groups** (or go to linkedin.com/groups) → **Create group**.
2. Upload the logo and cover, then fill in the fields below.
3. Discoverability: **Public**, so posts can be found and shared. Turn on **Require admin approval for posts** for the first few weeks to keep spam out.
4. Click **Create**. Then, in **Manage group → Settings**, link the group to the Basis Company Page if that option is shown.
5. Invite your own relevant connections from **Invite** (finance students, analysts, CFA candidates). Invite people you'd genuinely expect to join, a few dozen at a time.

>

## Habits that work on LinkedIn

- Post from your **personal profile** too. Founder posts reach far more people than new company pages. Reshare Page posts with one personal line on top.
- Put the link in the first comment rather than the post body when testing reach; compare both and keep what works.
- Reply to every comment in the first hour.
- Tag nobody who hasn't agreed to be tagged.
