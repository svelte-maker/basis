# WhatsApp kit

Use a **WhatsApp Community**: one announcement group (admins only, low noise) plus one or two chat groups. People join for reminders and the daily problem; keep it calm or they mute and leave.

## Before you start

- Ideally use the **WhatsApp Business** app on a dedicated number, so your personal number isn't the community contact. You can also run it from your personal account; members of the announcement group generally can't see each other's numbers, but group chat members can.
- Have the logo (square, 640 × 640 px works) ready on the phone.

## Create it

1. Open WhatsApp → **Communities** tab (Android: the communities icon at the top or bottom; iPhone: the Communities tab) → **New community** → **Get started**.
2. Name: **Basis Finance Community**. Description: see below. Add the logo. Tap the green arrow / **Create community**.
3. WhatsApp creates an **Announcements** group automatically. Only admins can post there; leave it that way.
4. Tap **Add group** → **Create new group**, and create:
   - **Basis · Lounge** (general chat, questions, introductions)
   - **Basis · Contest room** (open around Sunday contests for approach discussion after the contest ends)
   Start with just the Lounge if you prefer; add the Contest room once ~30 people are active.
5. Community settings (tap the community name → **Community settings**):
   - **Who can add groups:** Only community admins.
   - **Invite link:** turn on, then **Copy link**. This is the link you share.
6. Each chat group → group info → **Group settings**:
   - **Edit group settings:** Only admins.
   - **Send messages:** All members (Lounge); for the Contest room, set to Only admins during a live contest, then back to All after it ends.
   - **Approve new members:** on, for the first month.
7. Post the welcome message in Announcements and the rules in each group, then pin them (long-press the message → **Pin**).

## Lounge: pinned rules

> *Lounge rules*
> 1. Be useful and kind. Critique the model, not the person.
> 2. No contest answers while a contest is live. Everyone gets different numbers anyway.
> 3. No promotions, referral links or unsolicited DMs to members.
> 4. No confidential employer or deal information.
> 5. Educational only, not investment advice.
> 6. English please, so everyone can join in.
> Admins remove posts or members who break these. Report anything to an admin.

## Growing it

- Share the invite link in your LinkedIn post, Discord welcome channel and with friends one to one.
- **Never** add numbers to the group without the person's agreement, and never paste the invite link into unrelated groups. Both get numbers reported and banned.
- Remove anyone who spams on the first offence.
- Limit Announcements to one or two messages a day.
