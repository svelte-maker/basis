# Contest calendar

Kept four weeks ahead by the Community Manager agent. Svelte creates each contest at /admin from this list until scheduling is automated.

Weekly contests: Sundays 17:00 IST (11:30 UTC), 60 minutes, 4 problems (Easy, Medium, Medium, Hard), mixed tracks (Svelte, 23 Oct). Contest #1 is the exception: 75 minutes, 5 problems, unrated (plan in the project files, 23 Oct). Monthly themed contest: one per month, 90 minutes, 5 problems in one theme.

**Naming (Svelte, 23 Oct):** every contest gets a name with energy that still says what's being tested. Format: `Basis #<n>: <Name>` for weekly contests, `<Name>` plus the month theme for monthly ones. The name is two or three words, riffs on the round's finance theme or deal story (a term a practitioner would recognise, like "Leverage Season" for a buyout round), and reads well in a story post or a group chat. No emoji in the title, no memes that date in a month, nothing that sounds like investment advice or a get-rich promise, and never the same name twice. Propose it with the problems; Svelte can veto.
