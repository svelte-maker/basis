## 2. Problem of the week: Debt service coverage ratio

**Problem:** `/problems/debt-service-coverage` (Easy, Banking & Credit). It isn't in any contest on the calendar, so discussing it can't leak an answer.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* Debt service coverage ratio (Easy, Banking & Credit)
>
> **Why it matters:** this is the first number a lender checks. Loan agreements set a minimum DSCR as a covenant, and if the borrower drops below it, the lender can step in. Credit analysts, real estate investors and leveraged finance bankers all run it on day one.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** debt service is interest **plus** scheduled principal, not interest alone. DSCR = net operating income ÷ (interest + principal). Below 1.0x, the income can't cover the loan.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/debt-service-coverage
