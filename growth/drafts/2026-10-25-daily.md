> Four finance ideas from this week, each one a line you can say in an interview:
>
> 1️⃣ Put-call parity needs no volatility.
> C + PV(K) = P + S is a no-arbitrage result. A 1-year call at $10.45 (S = K = $100, 5% annual compounding) means the put must be $5.69.
>
> 2️⃣ You subtract cash to get enterprise value because the buyer gets it back.
> $5,000m equity + $1,500m debt − $500m cash = $6,000m EV.
>
> 3️⃣ WACC uses after-tax debt.
> 60% × 12% + 40% × 8% × (1 − 25%) = 9.60%. Skip the tax shield and you get 10.40%.
>
> 4️⃣ A quoted rate isn't the rate you pay.
> 3.5% a month is "42% a year", but the effective rate is 51.11%.
>
> Every one of these has a free lesson on Basis: https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/learn
