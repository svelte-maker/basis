> "Depreciation goes up by 10. Walk me through the three statements."
>
> It's probably the most-asked accounting question in finance interviews. Here's the answer in under a minute, assuming a 25% tax rate:
>
> **Income statement:** pre-tax income falls 10. Tax falls 2.5. Net income falls **7.5**.
>
> **Cash flow statement:** start with net income (−7.5), then add back depreciation (+10), because it isn't a cash payment. Cash goes **up by 2.5**.
>
> **Balance sheet:** cash +2.5, net PP&E −10, so assets fall 7.5. Retained earnings fall 7.5, so equity falls 7.5. It balances.
>
> The point interviewers are checking: depreciation is non-cash, but it isn't cash-*neutral*. The tax saving is real money.
>
> The three answers people give instead: "0" (forgot the tax), "−7.5" (forgot the add-back) and "+10" (added back without the net income hit).
>
