## 1. LinkedIn

- **Goal:** a saveable mental-maths tip, with the nuance most posts about it leave out.
- **Suggested time:** Sat 31 Oct, 11:00 IST
- **Link:** https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/learn/time-value-of-money

> The rule of 72 is the fastest maths in finance. It is also wrong in a predictable way.
>
> The rule: money doubles in about 72 ÷ (rate in %) years.
>
> Here's how close it gets, with the exact answer next to it:
>
> • 8%: rule says 9 years, exact is 9.01
> • 12%: rule says 6, exact is 6.12
> • 24%: rule says 3, exact is 3.22
> • 36%: rule says 2, exact is 2.25
>
> Near 8% it's almost perfect. The higher the rate, the more it overstates how fast you double. At 36%, ₹1 lakh after the rule's 2 years is ₹1.85 lakh, not ₹2 lakh.
>
> How to use it: for sanity checks in an interview or a meeting at normal rates, trust it. For high rates, add a little time.
>
> It also works backwards. Something that doubles in 6 years is compounding at about 12%.
