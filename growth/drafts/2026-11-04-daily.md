> ₹50 lakh home loan. 9% a year. 20 years. EMI: ₹44,986.
>
> After 10 years, exactly half the term, how much of the loan is still owed?
>
> A) About ₹25 lakh, since half the time means half the loan
> B) About ₹30 lakh
> C) About ₹35 lakh
>
> Pick one before you scroll.
>
> .
> .
> .
>
> It's **C: ₹35.51 lakh.** In those 10 years you'd pay ₹53.98 lakh in EMIs, but only ₹14.49 lakh of that reduces the loan. The rest, ₹39.50 lakh, is interest.
>
> Early EMIs are mostly interest because interest is charged on a balance that's still large. You don't get to half the loan until year 14.
>
> That's also why prepaying early matters so much. ₹5,000 a month extra ends this loan 4.5 years sooner and saves ₹15.17 lakh of interest.
