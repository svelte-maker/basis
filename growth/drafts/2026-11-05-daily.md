## 1. LinkedIn

- **Goal:** a clear explainer on why "safe" bonds can lose money, aimed at students and early-career readers.
- **Suggested time:** Thu 5 Nov, 17:30 IST
- **Link:** https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/learn/bonds-and-yields

> "Bonds are safe" depends a lot on which bond.
>
> Three bonds, all paying a 7% coupon, all priced at ₹1,000. Then rates rise from 7% to 8%:
>
> • 2-year bond: falls to ₹981.85 (**−1.81%**)
> • 10-year bond: falls to ₹932.05 (**−6.80%**)
> • 30-year bond: falls to ₹886.88 (**−11.31%**)
>
> Same coupon, same credit, same 1% move. The difference is time. The further away the cash flows, the more a change in the discount rate hits their value today. That sensitivity is **duration**: about 1.8, 7.1 and 12.5 here.
>
> One more twist: if rates instead fall 1%, the 30-year bond gains **+13.84%**, more than it loses on the way up. That asymmetry is **convexity**, and it's why long bonds rally hard when rates fall.
>
