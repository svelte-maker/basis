## 2. Problem of the week: Double-declining balance

**Problem:** `/problems/double-declining-balance` (Medium, Accounting). It isn't in any contest on the calendar.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* Double-declining balance, year 2 (Medium, Accounting)
>
> **Why it matters:** the depreciation method changes reported profit, the tax bill and the book value of assets, without changing a rupee of cash spent. Analysts comparing two companies with different methods have to adjust for it, and auditors check that the method fits how the asset actually wears out.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** the rate is twice the straight-line rate, and it's applied to what's *left* of the book value, not the original cost. So work out year 1 first.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/double-declining-balance
