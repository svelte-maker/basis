## 1. LinkedIn

- **Goal:** a clear accounting explainer for students and early-career analysts.
- **Suggested time:** Tue 10 Nov, 17:30 IST
- **Link:** https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/learn/free-cash-flow

> Two companies. Both report ₹500 crore of EBITDA. One generates 7x the cash of the other.
>
> Start from the same place: EBITDA 500, D&A 100, tax 25%. Both have ₹300 crore of after-tax operating profit.
>
> Then the cash goes out:
> • Company A spends ₹110 crore on capex and ₹10 crore more on working capital → **₹280 crore of free cash flow**
> • Company B, growing fast in a capital-heavy business, spends ₹300 crore on capex and ₹60 crore on working capital → **₹40 crore**
>
> EBITDA ignores both. That's why it's a useful starting point and a poor finishing point.
>
> Three questions to ask whenever someone quotes EBITDA:
> 1. How much capex does it take to keep it growing?
> 2. How much cash gets tied up in receivables and inventory?
> 3. What share of EBITDA actually turns into free cash flow? Here it's 56% vs 8%.
>
