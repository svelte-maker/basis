## 2. Problem of the week: Retained earnings roll-forward

**Problem:** `/problems/retained-earnings-rollforward` (Easy, Accounting). It isn't in any contest on the calendar.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* Retained earnings roll-forward (Easy, Accounting)
>
> **Why it matters:** retained earnings is the link between the income statement and the balance sheet. It's the first check an analyst runs when building a three-statement model, and when the roll-forward doesn't tie, it usually means a dividend, buyback or adjustment has been missed somewhere.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** start with the opening balance, add net income (which can be negative), and subtract dividends. Dividends never touch the income statement.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/retained-earnings-rollforward
