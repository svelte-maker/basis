## 2. Problem of the week: Gross and operating margin

**Problem:** `/problems/gross-margin` (Easy, Accounting). It isn't in any contest on the calendar.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* Gross and operating margin (Easy, Accounting)
>
> **Why it matters:** margins are how you compare a ₹200 crore business with a ₹2,000 crore one. Gross margin shows pricing power over the product itself. Operating margin shows whether the business is still profitable after paying to sell it and run it. It's often the first line in an equity research note.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** the question asks for *operating* margin, so take off both cost of goods sold and SG&A before you divide by revenue.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/gross-margin
