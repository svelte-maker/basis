**Where the numbers come from:** `irr()` and `effectiveAnnualRate()` in `src/lib/finance/tvm.ts`. The offer is **illustrative**, not any real lender's or retailer's terms. Cash flows from the buyer's side: you effectively borrow ₹60,000 but receive ₹58,500 after the fee, then repay ₹10,000 a month for 6 months.

| Case | Monthly IRR | Annual rate (×12) | Effective annual rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ₹1,500 fee, 6 months | 0.728% | 8.74% | **9.10%** |
| ₹1,500 fee + 18% GST on the fee (₹1,770), 6 months | 0.862% | 10.35% | **10.85%** |
| ₹1,500 fee, 12 months of ₹5,000 | 0.392% | 4.70% | **4.80%** |

Takeaways: the fee is only 2.5% of the price, but it's paid up front and the loan is repaid quickly, so the annual cost is several times higher. The shorter the tenure, the more a fixed fee costs per year. The drafts say "if GST applies" rather than asserting it for every offer, and never name a lender or retailer.
