## 2. Problem of the week: Effective annual rate

**Problem:** `/problems/effective-annual-rate` (Easy, Corporate Finance). It isn't in any contest on the calendar.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* Effective annual rate (Easy, Corporate Finance)
>
> **Why it matters:** the rate a lender quotes is rarely what you pay. A credit card, a personal loan and a fixed deposit can all show "12%" and cost or earn different amounts, depending on how often interest compounds. The effective rate is what lets you compare them.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** divide the APR by the number of periods, compound it for that many periods, then subtract 1.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/effective-annual-rate
