## 1. LinkedIn

**Goal:** saves and shares; a weekly round-up people bookmark.
**Suggested time:** Sun 22 Nov, 10:00 IST.
**Link:** https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/learn

> Four finance mistakes, and the one number that catches each. (All illustrative.)
>
> **1. Trusting the current ratio alone.**
> Two companies, both at 2.0x. Strip out inventory and one is at 1.8x, the other at 0.4x. The quick ratio tells you who can actually pay next month's bills.
>
> **2. Believing a 0% EMI is free.**
> A ₹1,500 fee on a ₹60,000 phone, repaid over 6 months, costs about 9.10% a year. The fee looks small because it's quoted against the price, not the time.
>
> **3. Discounting the wrong cash flow.**
> Free cash flow to the firm (110) goes with WACC. Free cash flow to equity (97.5) goes with the cost of equity. Swap them and the share price is wrong.
>
> **4. Using the textbook annuity formula for rent.**
> ₹1 lakh a year for 5 years at 8% is ₹3,99,271 if paid at year-end and ₹4,31,213 if paid in advance. Rent is paid in advance.
>
> Each one has a free lesson with a live sheet:
> basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/learn
>
> Which one have you seen in an exam or an interview?
>
