## 2. Problem of the week: Portfolio expected return

**Problem:** `/problems/portfolio-expected-return` (Easy, Portfolio & Risk). It isn't in any contest on the calendar, and it isn't on [#48](https://github.com/svelte-maker/basis/issues/48)'s list. I skipped depreciation because the Growth drafts covered it on 24 Nov.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* Portfolio expected return (Easy, Portfolio & Risk)
>
> **Why it matters:** every SIP, retirement fund and model portfolio is a mix of assets. Before you worry about risk, you need to know what the mix is expected to earn, and that comes down to how much of each you hold.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** it's a weighted average. Multiply each asset's expected return by its weight, then add them up.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/portfolio-expected-return
