> Interview question: "In your DCF, how much of the value comes from the terminal value?"
>
> If you haven't checked, the answer is usually "most of it."
>
> A plain 5-year DCF (illustrative, ₹ crore):
> Revenue 1,000 growing 10% a year · EBIT margin 20% · WACC 11% · terminal growth 4%
>
> PV of the 5 forecast years: 637
> PV of the terminal value: 1,859
> Enterprise value: 2,496
>
> **74.5% of the value sits beyond year 5**, in a single line of the model.
>
> And it's sensitive:
> → Terminal growth 3% instead of 4%: EV −9.9%
> → Terminal growth 5%: EV +13.2%
> → WACC 10% instead of 11%: EV +17.1%
>
> What good answers add:
> 1. Cross-check the terminal value against an exit multiple. This one implies 8.1x EBITDA. Is that sensible for the sector?
> 2. Show a WACC × growth sensitivity table, never a single number.
> 3. Keep terminal growth at or below long-run nominal GDP growth.
>
