## 2. Problem of the week: LBO sources and uses

**Problem:** `/problems/lbo-sources-and-uses` (Easy, Private Equity). It fits the Capital Stack theme, but it isn't in any contest on the calendar and it isn't on [#48](https://github.com/svelte-maker/basis/issues/48)'s list.

> 🧮 *Problem of the week:* LBO sources and uses (Easy, Private Equity)
>
> **Why it matters:** every buyout starts with one table. The money coming in (debt and the sponsor's equity) has to equal the money going out (the purchase price, fees and the cash the business needs on day one). Get that table right and the rest of the model follows.
>
> 💡 **Hint:** add up the uses first. Whatever the lenders don't cover is the sponsor's equity.
>
> Solve it (your own numbers): https://basis-psi-roan.vercel.app/problems/lbo-sources-and-uses
